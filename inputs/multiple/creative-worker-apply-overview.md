# Apply

[Read the full guidance before you apply.](/government/publications/guidance-on-application-for-uk-visa-under-tier-5-temporary-worker)

## Apply from outside the UK

You must [apply online for a Temporary Work - Creative Worker visa.](https://apply-to-visit-or-stay-in-the-uk.homeoffice.gov.uk/sort/start/temporary_work_out_uk)

### Proving your identity and providing supporting documents

You’ll either:

-   use the ‘UK Immigration: ID Check’ app to scan your identity document - you’ll also create or sign into your UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) account
-   have your fingerprints and photo (biometric information) taken at a [visa application centre](/find-a-visa-application-centre)

You’ll be told what you need to do when you apply.

If you do need an appointment:

-   you’ll need to take your passport with you to the visa application centre (you’ll be able to collect it on the day)
-   you may have to travel to get to your nearest centre (this could be in another country)

You may be able to pay to [get a faster decision](/faster-decision-visa-settlement).

## Apply from inside the UK

You can only [extend your existing visa](/creative-worker-visa/extend-your-visa) if you’re already in the UK.
